# Screen inventory

Browser UI screens. Each states purpose and key interactions.

## Board: lane view

- Purpose: see work by lane and status.
- Interactions: filter by tag, contributor, goal; drag to move; open item.

## Board: goal view

- Purpose: see progress by goal.
- Interactions: open goal; view its tickets and closure state.

## Work item detail

- Purpose: read and edit one item.
- Interactions: view capsule, criteria, constraints, dependencies, decisions, attempts, open questions; claim and release; attach evidence.

## Capsule viewer

- Purpose: read the assembled capsule for an item.
- Interactions: choose token budget; toggle sections; jump to source by ref; view omission manifest.

## Ledger viewer

- Purpose: inspect execution records and validity.
- Interactions: filter by work item, kind, validity; see why a record is stale; trigger re-validation.

## Evidence panel

- Purpose: review recorded observations and their validity.
- Interactions: promote inferred records; mark contradictions; open the producing execution.

## Review surface

- Purpose: approve or reject a change against criteria.
- Interactions: view diff, verdicts, evidence; leave a review note; close or reopen.

## Settings

- Purpose: configure identity, leases, sync, connected repos.
- Interactions: assume actor; set lease TTL; connect or disconnect a repo; manage goals.
